Title: Infinite Bunny

Description:
A golden bunny sits in a corridor between two parallel mirrors. The camera looks into
one of the mirrors at an angle, so the bunny repeats again and again, fading and blurring as the
copies recede into the reflection.

Technical achievements:
- Mirror reflection: an iterative bounce loop (up to 15 bounces) follows rays back and forth between
  the two mirror walls. Each bounce multiplies the throughput by the mirror's reflectivity (0.85),
  so every copy of the bunny is a little dimmer than the previous one.
- Depth of field: a thin-lens camera focused on the real bunny. Reflected copies travel a longer
  light path, so they fall further out of focus the deeper they are in the mirror.
- Soft shadows: a light above gives soft shadows under the bunny.

Artistic motivation:
While doing the reflection part, it reminded me of my elevator where it looks like I was in an 
infinite space. So I decided to put two mirrors facing each other, turning a single 
bunny into a whole line of them, and depth of field makes the reflections drift out of focus.
